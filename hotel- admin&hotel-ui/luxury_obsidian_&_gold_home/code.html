Our Heritage

A Legacy of
Golden Era Opulence

Founded by the visionary architect Arthur Golding, our hotel stands as a testament to the roaring twenties. Restored meticulously to preserve the original obsidian marble floors and gold-leaf archways, we invite you to step back into a time of unbridled elegance.

Every corner whispers stories of the dignitaries, artists, and royals who have walked these halls for over a century.
